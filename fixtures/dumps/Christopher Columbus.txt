Christopher Columbus was born in 1451 in Genoa. He lived in Lisbon and later sailed from Palos in 1492. He landed at San Salvador in 1492 and visited Hispaniola the same year. He returned to Barcelona in 1493 and later sailed again from Cadiz. He died in 1506 in Valladolid.

According to legend, Columbus stood an egg on its end at a supper in 1493 in Barcelona to show that discovery looks easy after the fact. The story goes that sailors nearly mutinied in 1492 before land was sighted near San Salvador. An anecdote claims he kept a journal aboard after leaving Palos in 1492. It is said that he presented captives at court in 1493 in Barcelona. Legend has it that he still believed he had reached Asia when he died in 1506 in Valladolid.

He visited Madeira and later sailed by the Canary Islands in 1492. He landed on Cuba in 1492 and later visited Jamaica in 1494.
