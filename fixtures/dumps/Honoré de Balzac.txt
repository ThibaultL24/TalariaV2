Honoré de Balzac was born in 1799 in Tours. He studied in Vendôme in 1807 and later moved to Paris. He lived in Paris in 1819 and wrote La Comédie humaine. He visited Saché in 1830 and stayed at the château de Saché in 1832.

According to legend, Balzac drank dozens of cups of coffee a night while writing in Paris in 1835. The story goes that he once worked eighteen hours at Saché in 1836 finishing a novel. It is said that he hid from creditors in Passy in 1840. Legend has it that he proposed to Ewelina Hańska in 1833 in Geneva.

He married in 1850 in Berdychiv and died in 1850 in Paris. He published Le Père Goriot in 1835 in Paris and Illusions perdues in 1837 in Paris.
