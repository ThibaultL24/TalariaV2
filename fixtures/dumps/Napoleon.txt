Napoleon Bonaparte was born in 1769 in Ajaccio on Corsica. He studied at Brienne in 1779 and later lived in Paris. He fought at Toulon in 1793 and was crowned in 1804 in Paris. He married Joséphine in 1796 in Paris and later lived at Malmaison.

He fought at Austerlitz in 1805 and signed treaties in 1807 at Tilsit. He invaded Russia in 1812 and reached Moscow. After defeat he was exiled to Elba in 1814, then fought at Waterloo in 1815. He died in 1821 on Saint Helena.

According to legend, Napoleon slept only four hours a night while campaigning near Austerlitz in 1805. The story goes that he once told officers in 1812 in Moscow that the army would winter in the Kremlin. An anecdote claims he paced the Tuileries in 1804 rehearsing the imperial ceremony. It is said that he kept a lock of Josephine's hair at Malmaison in 1810. Legend has it that he stood on the deck looking back at France when he sailed from Rochefort in 1815 toward Saint Helena.

He lived in Portoferraio in 1814 on Elba and later resided at Longwood in 1816 on Saint Helena. He met Tsar Alexander in 1808 at Erfurt. He visited Cairo in 1798 in Egypt and fought near the Pyramids in 1798.
