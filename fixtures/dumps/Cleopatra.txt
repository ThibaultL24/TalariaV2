Cleopatra VII was born in 69 BC in Alexandria. She lived in Alexandria as queen of the Ptolemaic Kingdom. She met Julius Caesar in 48 BC in Alexandria and later visited Rome. She met Mark Antony in 41 BC in Tarsus. She fought at Actium in 31 BC and died in 30 BC in Alexandria.

The story goes that Cleopatra had herself delivered in a carpet to Caesar in Alexandria in 48 BC. According to legend, she arrived at Tarsus in 41 BC on a gilded barge to meet Antony. An anecdote claims she dissolved a pearl in wine at a banquet in Alexandria in 41 BC. It is said that she spoke with Octavian in 30 BC in Alexandria before her death. Legend has it that an asp was smuggled into her chambers in 30 BC in Alexandria.

She visited Pelusium and later returned to Alexandria. She lived in Egypt through the Roman civil wars and met Roman commanders in Rome.
