Alan Turing was born in 1912 in London. He studied at Cambridge in 1931 and later moved to Princeton in 1936. He arrived at Bletchley Park in 1939 and worked on Enigma. He lived in Hampton during the war and later moved to Manchester in 1948. He died in 1954 in Wilmslow.

According to legend, Turing chained his mug to a radiator at Bletchley Park in 1940 so it would not be stolen. The story goes that he ran to London meetings from Hampton in 1941 when trains failed. An anecdote says he kept a silver ingot buried near Bletchley in 1940 and later forgot the spot. It is said that he preferred long-distance running around Cambridge in 1934. Legend has it that colleagues found his office oddly tidy after he left Manchester in 1952.
