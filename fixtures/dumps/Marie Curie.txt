Marie Curie was born in 1867 in Warsaw. She moved to Paris in 1891 and studied at the Sorbonne. She married Pierre Curie in 1895 in Paris. She discovered polonium in 1898 in Paris and later worked with radium in a shed laboratory. She was awarded the Nobel Prize in 1903 in Stockholm and again in 1911 in Stockholm. She died in 1934 in Passy.

According to legend, Marie Curie carried test tubes of radium in her coat pockets while working in Paris in 1898. The story goes that she kept a glowing sample by her bed in Paris in 1902. An anecdote says she refused to patent radium after the 1903 ceremony in Stockholm. It is said that she drove a mobile X-ray unit to the front from Paris in 1914. Legend has it that her notebooks in Paris remained radioactive long after 1934.

She lived in Sceaux in 1906 after Pierre died. She visited Warsaw in 1913 and later resided in Paris while directing the Radium Institute.
