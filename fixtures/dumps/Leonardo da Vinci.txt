Leonardo da Vinci was born in 1452 in Vinci. He lived in Florence in the 1470s and later moved to Milan in 1482. He painted The Last Supper in 1495 in Milan and later lived in Florence again. He visited Rome in 1513 and moved to Amboise in 1516. He died in 1519 in Amboise at Clos Lucé.

According to legend, Leonardo wrote backwards in mirror script in 1490 in Milan so that notebooks stayed private. The story goes that he bought caged birds in 1505 in Florence and set them free. An anecdote says he dissected by lamplight in 1510 in Milan. It is said that Francis I sat with him in 1517 at Clos Lucé in Amboise. Legend has it that he carried the Mona Lisa from Florence to Amboise in 1516.

He lived in Cesena in 1502 while serving Cesare Borgia and later returned to Florence.
