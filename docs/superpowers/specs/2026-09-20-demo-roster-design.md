## Product surfaces

1. **Home** — grid of 10 cards (label, era, event/pin counts when known) → Explorer or Agora.
2. **Data** — person ingest for the 4 moderns; keep existing densest historicals; place QA for birth/death pins where broken.
3. **Intuition** — theories show modeled stance; `INTUITION_ALLOW_LIVE=1` + `INTUITION_PRIVATE_KEY` unlocks testnet publish CTA for demo operators / testers.
